Product Description

This professional digital chess clock features 8 timing modes with 42 preset rules, including countdown, delay, increment, bonus time, and count-up options. Custom settings can be saved for easy reuse, while responsive rocker-style buttons ensure quick and accurate operation. Most timing parameters can be adjusted during play, and the built-in memory function retains settings after power-off. Additional features include move counting and mute mode, making it ideal for casual and tournament chess.

  • Model Number: PS-1688
  • Main material: ABS
  • Product colour: Blue
  • Power supply mode: DC 3V power supply (excluding two AA batteries)
